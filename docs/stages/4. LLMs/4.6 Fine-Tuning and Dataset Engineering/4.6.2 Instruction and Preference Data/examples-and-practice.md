## Check Your Understanding

| Question | What a strong answer includes |
|---|---|
| Why does Instruction and Preference Data matter? | It connects to an llm fundamentals notebook comparing models, tokenization, structured outputs, embeddings, costs, and failure cases. and names a practical risk. |
